SURAT ATELIER · EST. 2026

Where Lapidary Precision Meets Contemporary Indian Watchmaking

Maison Gujarat Time Company was founded upon an authentic premise: that Surat, having perfected the art of cutting nine out of ten diamonds on Earth with sub-micron patience, possesses the rightful metallurgical pedigree to build the world's most exacting mechanical timepieces.

CHAPTER I · THE SURAT CRUCIBLE

Sub-Micron Tolerances Forged in Diamond Dust

For over a century, master artisans in Surat have coaxed fire and brilliance from raw carbon crystals. This craft requires calculating angles to one-tenth of a degree and measuring tolerances in microns rather than millimeters.

Maison GTC redirects this generational obsession toward mechanical chronometry. When our master polishers apply a 45° anglage bevel to the bridges of Calibre GTC-2601, or execute poli noir (black polish) on our hand-finished steel hands, they deploy the very same diamond-paste techniques passed down through lineages of Gujarati gem-cutters.

Calibre GTC-2601 · Monobloc Rotor & 45° Hand-Polished Anglage
CHAPTER II · PHILOSOPHY & CADENCE

The Scarcity of Unhurried Mastery

In an era of automated mass production, Maison GTC chooses deliberate scarcity. We craft in numbered micro-allocations—most notably the Horizon 300 Genesis Series, strictly limited to three hundred individually registered timepieces worldwide.

Each case is cold-forged from medical-grade 316L high-density steel, and each movement undergoes a rigorous 7-day chronometric validation cycle in six positions and three temperatures before earning the Surat Atelier hallmark.

THE FOUNDATIONAL TRIAD

Three Foundational Pillars

PILLAR 01 // ARCHITECTURE

Monobloc Casework

Machined from solid billets of surgical 316L steel. Featuring alternating satin-brushed flank surfaces and mirror-polished bezels designed to endure extreme high-altitude atmospheric shifts.

PILLAR 02 // CHRONOMETRY

In-House Calibre GTC-2601

Operating at 28,800 vibrations per hour (4 Hz) with a 42-hour autonomy reserve. Equipped with anti-shock Incabloc protection and a custom skeletonized tungsten-ruthenium oscillating weight.

PILLAR 03 // PROVENANCE

Archival Ledger & Provenance

Every timepiece is recorded in the physical and cryptographic ledger at the Surat Atelier, backed by a lifetime immutable Certificate of Authenticity signed by our founding master horologists.
