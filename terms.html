❖ HOROLOGICAL CHARTER & ALLOCATION PROTOCOLS ❖

Terms of Allocation & Manufacture Service

These terms establish the legal and horological protocols governing the allocation, numbered edition reservation, armored custody transit, and archival provenance transfer of chronometers crafted by Gujarat Time Company Pvt. Ltd.

Terms Index
1. Genesis Allocation Limits 2. Escrow & Deposit Security 3. Armored Transit & Salon 4. Bespoke Inscriptions 5. Provenance Rights 6. Governing Jurisdiction
PROTOCOL 01

Genesis Allocation Limits (Horizon 300)

The foundational edition of the GTC Himalayan is strictly capped at three hundred (300) individually serialized timepieces globally. Under our manufacture charter, no supplementary batch or duplicate serial number will ever be struck under Ref. R 39 SC AT.

Chronological Priority: Timepiece allocations are confirmed in strict order of validated reservation. Patrons may designate a requested preferred number (#001 to #300), which will be locked upon verification of deposit.

PROTOCOL 02

Deposit & Manufacture Escrow Protection

Upon submitting an allocation request through the GTC portal or private concierge, the initial deposit is deposited into a dedicated escrow account.

Funds remain secured until the timepiece completes the full 7-Day ISO 3159 Chronometer Accuracy Protocol (-2 / +4 seconds per day) and passes double-gasket 10 ATM hydro-static chamber validation. Full balance settlement is executed prior to final armored dispatch or at the in-person Surat Salon handover.

PROTOCOL 03

Armored Courier Transit & Salon Handover

Patrons may select between two certified modes of acquisition:

  • Surat Atelier Private Salon Handover: The patron is received at the Surat Atelier for a personalized white-glove unboxing ceremony, complete with 10x loupe inspection and complimentary STV Airport VIP transfer.
  • Insured Armored Courier Dispatch: Dispatched via bonded armored security logistics with sealed tamper-evident security clasps, delivered exclusively into the hands of the certified patron upon biometric ID verification.
PROTOCOL 04

Caseback Engraving & Bespoke Commissions

Patrons may commission an inscriptional engraving on the 316L surgical steel caseback perimeter (e.g. initials, family motto, commemorative dates).

Prior to permanent diamond-graver execution in Surat, a 1:1 scale digital engineering proof will be dispatched to the patron for final signature. Once engraved, bespoke casework is non-cancelable.

PROTOCOL 05

Provenance Transfer & Archival Integrity

Each timepiece is delivered with an official Certificate of Authenticity signed by our Co-Founders and Master Horologist.

Should a patron elect to sell, gift, or bequeath the timepiece in the future, both parties may contact the Maison Registry (concierge@gujarattimecompany.com) to formally update the archival ledger, ensuring the new custodian receives ongoing 5-Year Warranty benefits.

PROTOCOL 06

Governing Law & Arbitration

These Terms of Allocation and any disputes arising therefrom shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The courts of Surat, Gujarat, shall have exclusive territorial jurisdiction over any proceedings relating to Maison Gujarat Time Company.
