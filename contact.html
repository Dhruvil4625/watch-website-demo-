CONCIERGE PROTOCOLS & PRIVILEGES

Private Viewing & Allocation Protocols

Frequently consulted guidelines regarding numbered allocations, private viewing appointments, and client discretion charters.

How are numbered allocations in the Horizon 300 series reserved?
Allocations are recorded sequentially on our archival ledger. Patrons may request specific allocation numbers (such as #007 / 300 or #042 / 300). Upon receiving your dossier request, our Chief Concierge places a 72-hour temporary reservation on the timepiece while personalized caseback engraving proofs and documentation are coordinated.
What takes place during a Private Salon Viewing & 10x Loupe Inspection?
In our private viewing sanctuary in Surat, you will be hosted for a 90-minute private session by our master horologist. You will inspect unencased Calibre GTC-2601 bridge anglage under 10x gemological magnification, observe chronometric regulation on diagnostic Witschi equipment, and review bespoke dial and strap pairings in natural daylight conditions.
Can international or interstate patrons arrange bespoke allocations remotely?
Yes. For patrons unable to travel immediately to Surat, our concierge conducts encrypted high-definition video sessions featuring real-time macro photography of individual serial-numbered pieces. Once approved, the commissioned chronometer is dispatched via dedicated armored courier with full worldwide transit insurance.
How does the Maison Discretion Charter protect client confidentiality?
Maison Gujarat Time Company operates under a strict discretion covenant. Patron identities, allocation registries, and transaction records are encrypted and held in offline air-gapped repositories. We do not publish collector registries or share patron records with third-party networks.
