WARRANTY ARTICLE 01

Scope of the 5-Year International Guarantee

Maison Gujarat Time Company warrants your GTC Himalayan timepiece against manufacturing, metallurgical, and assembly defects for a full period of five (5) years from the date of registered delivery.

What Is Fully Covered: Mechanical movement regulation, escapement failure, balance spring anomalies, rotor bearing wear, dial marker adhesion, hand alignments, and internal sapphire gasket hermetic seal failures.

The warranty does not cover normal cosmetic abrasions to external 316L steel surfaces over years of wear, accidental drop impacts beyond ISO 1413 specifications, or unauthorized tampering by non-certified third-party watchmakers.

WARRANTY ARTICLE 02

100-Meter (10 ATM) Hydro-Static Standards

The GTC Himalayan features a dual O-ring screw-down winding crown and a threaded 316L monobloc caseback hermetically sealed to 10 ATM (100 meters / 330 feet).

WARRANTY ARTICLE 03

Magnetic Shielding & Precision Defense

Calibre GTC-2601 is engineered with non-ferromagnetic balance spring alloys and is tested to resist magnetic fields up to 4,800 A/m under ISO 764.

However, direct close-proximity contact with powerful neodymium magnets (such as magnetic tablet covers, high-output stereo subwoofers, or magnetic resonance scanners) can temporarily induce magnetized balance coils. Should your timepiece gain sudden rapid seconds per day, simply dispatch it to our Surat Atelier for instant complimentary demagnetization.

WARRANTY ARTICLE 04

Daily Horological Care Guidelines

Adhering to simple care habits will ensure your GTC chronometer retains its pristine luster and chronometric precision across decades:

  • Cleansing: Wipe the casing and anti-reflective sapphire crystal with the microfiber cloth supplied in your handcrafted GTC presentation vault.
  • Manual Winding: If unworn for over 42 hours, unscrew the crown to position 1 and rotate clockwise 20 to 25 rotations to initiate the mainspring before setting time.
  • Leather Straps: Natural tanned calf saddlery should avoid prolonged water saturation. Clean with a dry cotton cloth and store in a ventilated environment.
WARRANTY ARTICLE 05

The Surat Atelier Servicing Journey

We recommend a complete manufacture service every five to seven years. When you send your timepiece to our Surat Atelier, it undergoes our 5-Step Master Overhaul:

  1. Diagnostic Inspection: Amplitude testing on a digital Witschi chronoscope in six positions.
  2. Ultrasonic Movement Cleansing: Complete calibre disassembly into its constituent components and ultrasonic degreasing bath.
  3. Gasket & Lubrication Renewal: Application of Swiss synthetic micro-lubricants (Moebius) to the escapement and gear trains.
  4. Chronometer Regulation: 7-day thermal calibration to guaranteed -2 / +4 sec/day accuracy.
  5. Return Handover: Re-issuance of a fresh 2-Year Service Guarantee and secure return courier.
