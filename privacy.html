Privacy Policy & Client Discretion

Maison Gujarat Time Company treats collector privacy as a foundational standard of haute horlogerie. We uphold rigorous cryptographic vault standards, strict non-disclosure of patron ownership, and absolute compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and GDPR.

Policy Sections
1. Pledge of Discretion 2. Data We Collect 3. Cryptographic Provenance 4. Transaction Security 5. Erasure & Transfer 6. Zero Tracking Telemetry 7. Data Protection Office
ARTICLE 01

Pledge of Absolute Collector Discretion

Maison Gujarat Time Company operates with private banking-grade confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, license, or barter client records, purchase histories, or allocation numbers to any marketing firm, data aggregator, or third party.

The Custodian Guarantee: Unless an explicit written waiver is executed by the patron (such as a public museum exhibition loan), no public ledger or social directory will ever disclose the identity of a Horizon 300 edition holder.

ARTICLE 02

Categories of Information Collected

We collect strictly the minimum personal information required to execute your horological reservation, customize your timepiece, and ensure secure physical handover:

  • Patron Identification: Full legal name, formal title, contact email, and mobile telephone for encrypted concierge communication.
  • Transit & Salon Coordinates: Residential or vault delivery address for armored courier dispatch, or arrival details for STV Airport valet reception.
  • Horological Customization: Caseback engraving inscriptional proofs, preferred allocation serial numbers (#001 to #300), and custom strap dimensions.
  • Archival Provenance: Cryptographic public keys and warranty registration records linked to your timepiece's individual movement calibre serial.
ARTICLE 03

Cryptographic Provenance Protection

Every GTC Himalayan chronometer is linked to an immutable SHA-256 cryptographic hash attested by our founding horologists. This hash binds the movement serial, casing metallurgical lot, and patron certificate without exposing personal identity on public networks.

Provenance records are maintained in isolated, tamper-evident cold-storage databases with strict multi-key authorization protocols.

ARTICLE 04

Transaction & Escrow Security

All financial transactions—whether conducted via bank wire, RTGS/NEFT, or encrypted credit gateway—are secured through 256-bit TLS encryption conforming to PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

Maison GTC does not store or process raw credit card or banking PIN credentials on its servers. All reservation escrow accounts are overseen by authorized banking institutions in Surat, Gujarat.

ARTICLE 05

Right of Access, Transfer & Erasure

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), patrons retain complete custody over their data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may at any moment request a digital transcript of your complete provenance ledger.
  • Right to Transfer Provenance: Upon secondary sale or inheritance of your timepiece, ownership records can be formally transferred in the Maison registry upon joint confirmation.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of ancillary marketing and communication records, preserving only statutory warranty and fiscal compliance logs.
ARTICLE 06

Zero Behavioral Tracking & Cookies

Our web platform does not employ third-party behavioral trackers, cross-site advertising beacons, or surveillance pixels. We use solely essential, first-party session tokens necessary to:

  • Preserve your active timepiece acquisition bag (`gtc_collector_cart`).
  • Authenticate your secure sign-in session to the Collector Sanctuary (`profile.html`).
  • Remember client telemetry preferences for 3D Three.js WebGL rendering.
